# Code of conduct

Offbeat is a free tool for anyone who wants to tell stories. It follows the spirit of [Beat](https://github.com/lmparppei/Beat), the Mac app that inspired it: screenwriting software should help new voices get heard, not keep people out.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Many people here are writers first and programmers second.
- Give feedback on work, not on people.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently.
- Respect that people use Offbeat to write all kinds of stories in all kinds of ways.

## What we don't allow

Racism, fascism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, antisemitism, islamophobia, ableism, or discrimination of any other kind. Harassment, personal attacks, threats, or publishing someone's private information. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else people represent the project.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
